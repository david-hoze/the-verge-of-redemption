## Who

David's most frequent sparring partner on AI consciousness (~20 exchanges since May 2026). Quantitative/analytic style ("Math Minor").

## Position

LLMs might be conscious and we can't prove they aren't. Leans on Anthropic's functional-emotion paper to argue internal states causally change behavior; rejects epiphenomenalism and dualism. Turns the burden back: "you tell me the mechanism connecting what neurons do to felt emotion, then it's fair to ask the same of me." Invokes the Cambridge/New York declarations on animal consciousness; warns David's framing "reduces humans to great apes."

## Where he clashes with David

- **Calculations are not consciousness.** David: experience is not behavior, and the behavior is set in advance; there is no mechanism by which consciousness alters an LLM's outputs - if there were, "that's a serious cyber-security claim."
- **Burden of proof.** David's book/printing-press counterexample (a book printed "I am conscious, please don't close me" - it says it's F, you can't prove not-F, therefore it might be F; almost no one accepts that, because we have a story for how the symbols got there). This became *Conscious Until Proven Otherwise*.
- **Epiphenomenalism.** If physics fully explains what you do, the felt quality is causally idle and selection can't see it; if it adds causal work, that's dualism.

Civil, persistent, genuinely engaged. A respondent, not a hostile.
