## Who

Thoughtful, precise interlocutor on AI trust and moral agency (~5 exchanges since May 2026). Computer-science register.

## Position

An LLM likely isn't a moral agent - "because it does not act, it just is" (a mathematical map from input to a next-token distribution). But an LLM-*based* system is more than the LLM, and it does act. Baseline: no trust for an AI - yet open to the possibility that a specific entity, or a shared design, could earn a higher baseline over time. "Humans are extremely easy to hack."

## Where he meets / diverges from David

Substantial common ground (no default trust; LLM not a moral agent). David pushes back on two points: **human-in-the-loop is non-negotiable** at high stakes, and **humans are not that easily hacked** when they have an incentive - and crucially can't be hacked at scale/predictably the way AI can. Respectful disagreement; a strong threshold candidate for the moral-status / agency thread.
