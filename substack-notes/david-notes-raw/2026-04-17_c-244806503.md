---Five hours. They gave you five hours between being cut open and being told to stand up. Your baby was in a room you couldn't walk to. Your body was shaking from something nobody explained. And you went anyway - shuffling down that hallway, held together by nothing, because your child was on the other side of it. I don't know who told you that was the easy way out. Those are the body remembering what the mind is still trying to put words around. You deserved someone next to you in that hallway. You deserved someone saying: I see what this is taking from you.
