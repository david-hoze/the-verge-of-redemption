---This resonated with me. The idea that the body already knows its rhythm and the work is listening rather than forcing - that maps to something I've experienced through my own tradition.

The Jewish calendar is lunar. Every month begins with the new moon. There's a daily rhythm (three prayer times following the sun), a weekly rhythm (six days of work, one complete day of rest), a monthly rhythm (celebrating the new moon - traditionally a women's day), and a yearly rhythm (festivals cycling with the seasons). It's cycles all the way down.

The part that surprised me when I started living inside it: I didn't have to figure out the rhythm. It was already there. The calendar did the listening for me until I learned to hear it myself. Something about having the structure given rather than designed made it easier to actually surrender to it - which sounds like what you're pointing toward with Wu Wei.
