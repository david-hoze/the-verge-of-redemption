---You're right that effort without direction is a hamster wheel. And the RAS point is interesting - what you're primed to see shapes what you find.

Where I'd push: "the only hard work in life is the inner work." In my experience, it's the opposite. The hardest work is showing up when you don't feel like it, being kind when you're depleted, keeping a commitment when every part of you wants to quit. That outer work reshapes the inner world - not the other way around. "We will do, and then we will understand." The doing comes first.
