---"Decision debt." That's the best name I've heard for something I've watched cost people years. Not the dramatic failures - the quiet ones. The year that looks identical to the last one because three decisions are sitting in a drawer.

Number 2 is the one that actually runs the show. Because the identity isn't just a label - it's a ceiling. I've watched people with real businesses, real revenue, real clients still introduce themselves like they're apologizing for being in the room. The business was ready two years ago. They just hadn't given themselves permission to match it.

The fact that you can see this pattern in other people and name it this cleanly means you've already walked through it yourself. That's not coaching from a textbook. That's someone who earned the clarity the hard way and decided to hand it back.
