---"When housing is scarce, speech is not free." That's the line nobody writes because it costs too much to admit. The landlord didn't say "we don't want you here." He said "introduce yourselves." And that's worse - because it sounds reasonable. It sounds like community. But you and I both know what it actually means: prove you're safe. Prove you're harmless. Attach a photo so they can see what kind of foreign you are. Surveillance dressed as courtesy. And you couldn't say no - because your family needed a roof. That's the machinery. It doesn't need to be cruel. It just needs to make you choose between your dignity and your children's stability. And then call the result integration.
