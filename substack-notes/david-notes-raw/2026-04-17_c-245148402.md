---The orchestra metaphor is the one that stays. Not the salad - that's just diversity. The orchestra is something else: each instrument sounds incomplete alone. The trumpet doesn't make sense without the timpani. The violin needs the silence between the other instruments to be heard. That's not "individuals contributing to a whole." That's individuals who can only become fully themselves inside the whole. The name needs the number. The number needs the name. Rashi's stars aren't just counted - they're called by name. Both at once. Beautiful dvar Torah.
