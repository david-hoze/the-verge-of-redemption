---"Feelings are weather - passing over the wide field of me, never staying long enough to become the earth itself." That's a real line. The distinction between what moves through you and what you are - most people collapse those two things. They feel the storm and think they are the storm. You're watching it cross the field and letting it go. That's not detachment. That's knowing the difference between the sky and the ground. The ground stays.
