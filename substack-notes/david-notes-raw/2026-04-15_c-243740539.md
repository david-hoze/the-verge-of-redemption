---“**In place of a hermeneutics we need an erotics of art.**” — Susan Sontag

Feel first. Interpret later.

Let it land in the senses and the heart before the mind rushes in to translate it.

This is the Ecstasy of Saint Teresa. An angel about to pierce her heart with a golden arrow. Her expression suspended between agony and transcendence, so close to each other you can't tell them apart.

This was commissioned to depict a saint's divine vision. But Bernini wasn't interested in depiction. He wanted you to feel what Teresa felt, before belief has anything to say about it. Sacred beauty doesn't ask for faith. It pierces, like the angel's arrow, regardless of what you believe.
