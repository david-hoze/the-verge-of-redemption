---There's a new economics paper asking: what if AI layoffs are good for each company but bad for everyone?

The logic: when you cut workers, you keep the savings. But those workers stop spending. And that lost spending mostly hurts your competitors — not you. So everyone keeps cutting.

It's called a "demand externality". Fancy term for: no single company feels the full cost of the customers they're helping to erase.

The paper says UBI won't fix it. Upskilling won't fix it. Only taxing automation would.
