---The thing that makes Publix work is the thing that makes it impossible to copy. No layoffs in 95 years isn't a policy - it's a covenant. Employee ownership since 1930 isn't a program - it's an inheritance. Every leader rising from within isn't a talent strategy - it's a tradition. The word "masterclass" implies you can extract these principles and teach them. But the 5% turnover exists precisely because none of it was ever extracted. It was lived, unbroken, for 95 years. The ingredients aren't the prescription - the kitchen is. I wrote about why that distinction matters here: [https://davidhoze.substack.com/p/the-prescription-nobody-filled](https://davidhoze.substack.com/p/the-prescription-nobody-filled)
