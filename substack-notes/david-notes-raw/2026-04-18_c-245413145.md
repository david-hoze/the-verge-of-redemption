---You organized overwhelm into five senses and gave each one a prescription. That's beautifully structured. But here's what I noticed: the structure itself is doing the thing you're writing about. You took the chaos of too-much-everything and sorted it into categories with actionable steps - which is exactly what a nervous system does when it can't handle the mess. The list is the remedy AND the symptom.

The breathwork at the end is where you stopped being a glossary and started being honest. 4-6-8. No essential oils required. No decluttering. Just your lungs and a count. You buried the simplest, most powerful tool at the bottom, after the vetiver and the dark chocolate, almost like you didn't trust it to be enough on its own.

It is. The rest is beautiful packaging. But the breath was always the thing.
