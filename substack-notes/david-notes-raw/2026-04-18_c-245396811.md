---"Nearly half my life became a taboo topic overnight." That sentence carries more weight than the rest of the piece combined. Because that's what nobody tells you about moving on - it doesn't just ask you to build something new. It asks you to pretend the old thing didn't shape you. And you refused.

Moving the photos instead of removing them - that's not a compromise. That's a woman who understands that her children's memory of their father lives inside those frames. You didn't keep them for yourself. You kept them so your kids never have to wonder if the first half of their story was something to be ashamed of.

"My whole life, not just some of it." That's the bravest decorating decision I've ever heard. Most people curate their homes to show who they are now. You built one that shows who you've been. Your kids are going to grow up in a house that tells the truth, and they'll learn from that long before they learn it from words.
