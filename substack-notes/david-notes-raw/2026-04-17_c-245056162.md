---"Not bound by force, nor sharp, nor loud, but soft as mist, and just as proud." That's the witch most people don't write about. Not the one with power over nature - the one who disappears into it. Stitched into it. The distinction between commanding the forest and being spoken through by it is the whole poem. You chose the second one. That's the harder magic. The kind that requires giving up the throne, not sitting on it.
