---The retelling. That's the part nobody warns you about. Every new room, every new nurse, you become the narrator of the worst day of your life on repeat - and each time you tell it, you have to stay clinical while your body is screaming. You're not a daughter in that moment. You're a translator between the person you love most and a system that needs her reduced to a chart.

And then you called her Mommy. And for one second you weren't the strong one holding it together. You were the kid who needs her mother to be okay. That's the most honest thing a grown woman can say in a hospital hallway.
