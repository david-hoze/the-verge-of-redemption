---The thing nobody says out loud: the reason parents panic during a regression isn't about the child. It's about them. The child wakes up screaming and the parent's nervous system reads it as "you failed." Not because they're bad parents - because they care so much that any disruption feels like proof they got it wrong. And that fear - that quiet, furious fear of not being enough - is what makes them tear down the plan and start over. The child was fine. The child was growing. The parent was the one who needed steadying. Your line landed: "The plan didn't stop working. The conditions around it changed." I'd add - the parent changed too. They got scared. And scared people rebuild things that didn't need rebuilding.
