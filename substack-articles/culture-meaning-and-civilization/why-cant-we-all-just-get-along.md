# Why Can't We All Just Get Along

*Mars Attacks! (1996). Directed by Tim Burton. Starring Jack Nicholson, Glenn Close, Pierce Brosnan. The Martians circle the Earth announcing "we come in peace" and vaporizing everyone who believes them, while humanity argues over what the signals really mean. The President responds with the speech of his life: why can't we all just get along? The ambassador sheds a tear, applauds, and vaporizes him too.*

The world today is flowing with information. The news, social media, independent reporters. The more I try to find out the truth, the more confused I get. Some people claim there are war crimes in Gaza, some claim the opposite. Some claim that there is a satanic cult running the world. Others claim there isn't. Some claim that the war on Iran is fake, some claim it's real, but it's an inner struggle between the elites. Some claim there are UFOs. Some claim it's a distraction. Some even claim that the world is flat.

I can tell you that for sure, the world isn't flat. But as to the other things? Since the Epstein files, nobody knows. So many names redacted, so many weird things happening. Jim Carrey is seen at a ceremony, and it's clear to everyone that it's a double, and not a very good one. The eyes are green instead of brown, and the face is very, very different. Other celebrities testifying, testimonies of real people. What on round earth is going on?

And why does the government release the Epstein files? And why only part of it? If I was trying to hide myself, I wouldn't release any of it. If assassinations are so easily done, all they have to do is just take out a few people, and they're done. So why all of a sudden they become so benevolent and decide to release part of it?

## The truth will be absent

At the end of tractate Sotah, the Hebrew sages describe the days before the Messiah comes, and many believe these are the days we live in. One of the signs there is "And the truth will be absent". In Hebrew there is another meaning for the word absent. Its meaning is herds. The truth will be in herds. One herd believes this, the other believes that. And there will be no way to tell who is right.

If I analyze the situation, taking an overview, I see a pattern. I think that whoever is controlling the media, is flooding the world with misinformation. If everyone is fed with different information, we are turned against each other, and we have no way of telling who is right. Some may prefer independent reporters to mainstream media, but who is to say they are not implanted? Some prefer testimonies, or personal accounts, but what's the guarantee they're not being paid?

I think the safest bet is to say, "I don't know **anything**". That's why I never engage in these discussions. I can only testify to what I saw with my own eyes, and was told by people I trust. No more than that.

## How can I get myself out of this maze?

So what do we do? We all want security, justice. We sometimes amplify the opinions we decide to trust, thinking this will promote the truth. I think it doesn't. I think it amplifies the disinformation, and we all remain confused.

So what *can* we do? There is a basic truth, one that isn't in the news, isn't on any social platform. This is it:

**We are all basically good people, that just want to live our lives in peace.**

All the citizens of the world, each and every one, is just a simple person trying to get by. He supports his government because of a narrative he's sold, but basically we are all peaceful people, and we just want to live peaceful lives.

So actually, what are we fighting about? Is it land? There's plenty of land. No simple person wakes up in the morning wanting his neighbor's field. Even here, in the land everyone is supposedly fighting over, ask the simple man on either side what he actually wants. He wants to go to work and raise his kids in peace. The land is the flag they wave over the fight, not the reason for it. Is it money? Maybe, but whoever earns, it's not us. Is it justice? Well, two wrongs don't make a right.

Friends. We are fighting over nothing.

## Why it doesn't help to know who is right

People are arguing all over the web about the situation in Gaza. Well, maybe there are war crimes committed in Gaza. Maybe there aren't. The sure thing is, someone is trying to make us argue over these things, when in fact, it doesn't lead to any solution.

Because the solution is never to decide who is right, and make more wars. More killing. Even if there is a genocide in Gaza, what should the world do? Dismantle Israel? Take over? Nobody's willing to do that. And I doubt if Israel will peacefully agree.

If we analyze the situation, we get to a clear conclusion. If Israel is depicted as an arch villain (and maybe it is), the nations will eventually go to war on Israel. I'm guessing atomic bombs will be used, and we are facing an apocalypse.

So it really doesn't help anyone to know who is right.

## So what do we do?

I have a dream. In my dream, all the soldiers of the world take down their weapons, and destroy them. They do it all at once. They go back to their homes and families, and everybody in the world decides to **refuse to fight**. For any reason.

The only thing holding us back is that we need to agree that all we want is peace. If we establish trust, and stop believing the narratives we are sold, we may advertise this new treaty, this new alliance. We don't even need to overthrow our governments. A government can't fight a war that nobody shows up to. We just need to refuse to take part in this blood bath.

Who's with me?
