# Why Calling Someone an Anti-Semite is Wrong

There is increasing criticism of the affairs going on in Israeli warfare. There is also increasing evidence of corruption in the most high places of the Israeli and US governments. I'm not here to discuss these matters, they are thoroughly discussed and I'm not an expert. However, there is also a rise in claims about the Jewish people itself. From gathering evidence on their conduct in European countries in the past, may it or may not be true, to current events.

Some people say it's wrong to base any claims on grounds of culture or religion. I think it's wrong. there may be true claims that do involve certain cultures, or religions. Why wouldn't there be? Culture and religion shape the minds of the people that are in them, why should these claims be automatically dismissed? Sure, some of them are just wrong, but I feel that the lack of discussion and the automatic dismissal just makes them more powerful, since the people holding them have no other source that confronts with these claims.

This causes a lot of despair, for people that really care about what's going on in the world. They might hear claims and evidence that the Jewish people are in fact responsible for a lot of problems in our society, and they are silenced when they try to talk about it. I don't think these people are crazy, or in some way bad people. They might be misinformed, but calling them Anti-Semitic and silencing the discussion just make things worse.

Take for instance the situation in Europe. People are not allowed to talk about Muslim culture and its effects on European countries, now filled with new immigrants from Islamic nations. They are different, they have a different culture, and a different religion, and it has its effects. Ignoring it makes it worse for the European citizens, and for the immigrants. I believe this situation will erupt in the end, and there will be unfortunate consequences. They have anthropologists, psychologists, law makers and philosophers at their dispense. Why not raise real questions, and make studies in the matter? I know that ideologies can get really extreme, and may end up committing crimes against humanity. But we have the capacity to think of laws that are fair, and find solutions that protect the European countries from being used, while respecting the immigrants.
