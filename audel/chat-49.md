Faygi has rage attacks - screaming and biting immediately. Sara (1.5 years younger) does the same.

**ChatGPT:**

Normalized the behavior for young, sensitive children. Provided:

1. **Understanding the source:** Overwhelm from frustration, fatigue, hunger, sensory overload. Faygi already dealing with inadequacy feelings and anxiety. Sara likely copying Faygi's patterns.
2. **During an attack:** Calm, steady presence. "I'm here with you. This is hard right now. We don't hurt. It will pass soon." Gently prevent physical harm without reacting in anger.

4. **Clear non-violent boundaries:** After the attack: "You're not alone. It's hard. But in our house we don't bite. When you want help, tell me in words or show me with your hand."
5. **When to seek professional help:** If attacks are very frequent, violent, or disrupting household routine.
