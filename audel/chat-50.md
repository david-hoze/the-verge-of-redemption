**ChatGPT:**

Explained carpal tunnel anatomy - narrow area where tendons and median nerve pass. Hormonal changes, repetitive holding positions, and hand-kneading cause swelling and tingling (usually in thumb, index, middle fingers).

Detailed 5-minute daily exercise routine:
1. Gentle wrist stretch (palm up, pull fingers back) - 15 sec x 3
2. Reverse stretch (palm down) - 15 sec x 3
3. "Butterfly kiss" (backs of hands together, press gently) - opens carpal tunnel
4. Finger opening with rubber band - 10 reps x 2/day
5. Hand shaking for drainage

Aids: night splint for carpal tunnel, good nursing pillow, alternate hands when lifting baby. Supplements: vitamin B6 (check with doctor), arnica or magnesium oil massage before bed.
